About Us

Learn more about Lesedi la Batho and our work in the community.

Who We Are

Lesedi la Batho is a community organisation focused on creating positive change and supporting people in need.

Our organisation works with community members, volunteers and partners to improve the lives of families and young people.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower communities through support, education, skills development and opportunities for growth.

Our Main Areas

Community Support

Providing assistance to vulnerable people and families.

Skills Development

Helping people learn skills that can improve their opportunities and independence.

Youth Development

Supporting young people through mentorship and development programmes.
